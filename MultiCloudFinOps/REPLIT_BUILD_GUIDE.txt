3. CLOUD PROVIDER ORGANIZATIONAL HIERARCHIES (Critical — Read First)
================================================================================

IMPORTANT: Each cloud provider has a DIFFERENT organizational structure.
The existing Huawei dashboard uses "Tenants" but that is Huawei-specific.
The dashboard must use the CORRECT hierarchy and terminology for each provider.

The dropdown in the header (currently "Tenant Selector") and the management
page (currently "Tenant Management") must adapt to each provider's real
organizational structure. The FinOps dashboard shows cost data scoped to
the provider's billing/organizational unit.

=== AWS ORGANIZATIONAL HIERARCHY ===

    AWS Organization (root)
    ├── Management Account (payer account — pays consolidated bill)
    ├── OUs (Organizational Units) [optional]
    │   ├── OUs (nested)
    │   │   └── Member Accounts
    │   └── Member Accounts
    └── Member Accounts (directly under root)
        └── Resources (EC2, S3, RDS, etc.)

KEY CONCEPTS:
- AWS Organizations: Service to consolidate multiple AWS accounts
- Management Account (Payer Account): Single account that creates the
  organization and receives the consolidated bill for ALL member accounts.
  Cannot be changed. Has full admin control.
- Organizational Units (OUs): Optional containers for grouping accounts.
  Can represent departments, environments, subsidiaries. Nestable.
  OUs are for governance (SCPs), NOT a billing construct —
  you cannot get a bill per OU directly.
- Member Accounts: The fundamental unit of isolation. Each has its own
  resources, IAM users/roles, and a unique 12-digit account ID.
  Resources always live inside an account scoped to a region.
- Consolidated Billing: Management account gets one bill aggregating
  all member accounts. Usage across accounts combines for volume
  discounts, RI sharing, and Savings Plans sharing.

BILLING SCOPE: Per Account. OUs are NOT a billing scope. You filter
by Account ID or by cost allocation tags in Cost Explorer.

FINOPS DASHBOARD MAPPING:
- Header dropdown: "Account Selector" → shows "All Accounts" + list
  of member accounts (e.g., "prod-account-001", "dev-account-002")
- Management page: "Account Management" (not "Tenant Management")
- Sidebar label: "Accounts" (not "Tenants")
- The "All Accounts" view = consolidated Organization view
- Clicking a specific account = scoped to that account's costs

=== AZURE ORGANIZATIONAL HIERARCHY ===

Azure has TWO parallel hierarchies: Resource Management and Billing.

RESOURCE MANAGEMENT HIERARCHY:
    Entra ID Tenant (identity boundary — formerly Azure AD)
    └── Root Management Group (auto-created, ID = tenant ID)
        ├── Management Groups [optional]
        │   ├── Management Groups (nested)
        │   │   └── Subscriptions
        │   └── Subscriptions
        └── Subscriptions (directly under root)
            └── Resource Groups
                └── Resources (VMs, Storage, Databases, etc.)

BILLING HIERARCHY (varies by agreement type):

Enterprise Agreement (EA):
    EA Billing Account (Enrollment)
    ├── Departments [optional]
    │   └── Enrollment Accounts
    │       └── Subscriptions
    └── Enrollment Accounts (directly)
        └── Subscriptions

Microsoft Customer Agreement (MCA):
    MCA Billing Account
    └── Billing Profiles (invoices generated here)
        └── Invoice Sections
            └── Subscriptions

KEY CONCEPTS:
- Entra ID Tenant: The foundational identity boundary. Every subscription
  trusts exactly one Entra ID tenant. This is the identity plane.
- Management Groups: Hierarchical method for organizing subscriptions.
  Policy and RBAC cascade down. Nestable.
- Subscriptions: The FUNDAMENTAL unit of billing, access control, and
  resource provisioning. Each subscription has a unique GUID. This is
  where costs are primarily tracked. Service limits are per-subscription.
- Resource Groups: Logical containers for related resources within a
  subscription. Resources in a group share lifecycle.
- Resources: The actual Azure services (VMs, Storage Accounts, etc.)
- Departments (EA) / Invoice Sections (MCA): Billing grouping constructs.
- Azure Cost Management: Supports BOTH resource scopes (Management Group,
  Subscription, Resource Group) and billing scopes (Billing Account,
  Department, etc.)

BILLING SCOPE: Per Subscription (primary). Also viewable per Management
Group, Resource Group, or billing constructs (Department/Invoice Section).
Tags provide additional cost allocation.

FINOPS DASHBOARD MAPPING:
- Header dropdown: "Subscription Selector" → shows "All Subscriptions"
  + list of subscriptions (e.g., "Prod-Sub-001", "Dev-Sub-002")
- Management page: "Subscription Management" (not "Tenant Management")
- Sidebar label: "Subscriptions" (not "Tenants")
- The "All Subscriptions" view = consolidated Management Group view
- Clicking a specific subscription = scoped to that subscription's costs

=== GCP ORGANIZATIONAL HIERARCHY ===

GCP has TWO separate hierarchies: Resource and Billing.

RESOURCE HIERARCHY:
    Organization (tied to Google Workspace / Cloud Identity domain)
    ├── Folders [optional]
    │   ├── Folders (nested)
    │   │   └── Projects
    │   └── Projects
    └── Projects (directly under org)
        └── Resources (Compute Engine, Cloud Storage, BigQuery, etc.)

BILLING HIERARCHY (separate from resource hierarchy):
    Cloud Billing Account (exists outside resource hierarchy)
    ├── Linked Projects (each project linked to exactly one billing account)
    └── Billing Subaccounts [resellers only]
        └── Linked Projects

KEY CONCEPTS:
- Organization: Root node of GCP resource hierarchy. Auto-provisioned when
  a Google Workspace or Cloud Identity account creates a GCP project.
  Tied to a specific domain (e.g., example.com).
- Folders: Optional grouping. Can represent departments, teams, environments.
  Nestable. IAM and Org Policies inherit from parents.
- Projects: The FUNDAMENTAL organizational unit in GCP. Every resource
  belongs to exactly one project. Each project has a globally unique
  project ID (immutable) and project number. Each project is linked to
  exactly one Cloud Billing Account.
- Resources: The actual GCP services. Always exist within a project.
- Cloud Billing Account: Exists OUTSIDE the resource hierarchy. Linked to
  projects and pays for their usage. Can pay for projects across different
  folders or even across organizations. Has a payment method via Google
  Payments Profile.
- Labels: Key-value pairs on resources forwarded to billing. The mechanism
  for sub-project cost allocation.
- BigQuery Billing Export: Primary mechanism for detailed cost analysis.

BILLING SCOPE: Per Project (primary). Also viewable per Folder (in
billing reports hierarchy view), per Label, or per Billing Account.

FINOPS DASHBOARD MAPPING:
- Header dropdown: "Project Selector" → shows "All Projects"
  + list of projects (e.g., "prod-web-app", "data-analytics-prod")
- Management page: "Project Management" (not "Tenant Management")
- Sidebar label: "Projects" (not "Tenants")
- The "All Projects" view = consolidated Billing Account / Org view
- Clicking a specific project = scoped to that project's costs

Budget: Each project has its own budget allocation.
Efficiency score: Per-project optimization score.

=== HUAWEI CLOUD ORGANIZATIONAL HIERARCHY ===

    Organization
    └── Root OU (auto-created)
        ├── OUs [optional]
        │   ├── OUs (nested)
        │   │   └── Member Accounts
        │   └── Member Accounts
        └── Member Accounts (directly under root)
            ├── Enterprise Projects (cross-region resource grouping)
            │   └── Resources
            └── "default" Enterprise Project (unassigned resources)

BILLING:
    Enterprise Center
    └── Master Account (Management Account / Payer)
        └── Unified Accounting
            ├── Member Account A (bills paid by master)
            ├── Member Account B (bills paid by master)
            └── Member Account C (bills paid by master)

KEY CONCEPTS:
- Organizations: Service for managing multiple Huawei Cloud accounts.
  Hierarchical tree with root OU at top.
- Management Account (Master Account): Creates the organization, pays
  consolidated bills via Enterprise Center.
- OUs: Containers for grouping member accounts. Represent departments,
  subsidiaries, teams. Nestable.
- Member Accounts: Created within or invited to the organization.
  Each retains its own resource management.
- Enterprise Projects: Cross-region logical grouping of resources within
  an account. This is the RECOMMENDED approach (replacing IAM projects).
  Resources can be transferred between enterprise projects. Supports cost
  tracking per enterprise project.
- Enterprise Center: Console for managing discounts, funds, invoices, and
  bills across multiple accounts. Unified accounting = master pays all.
- Cost Center: Provides cost analysis/allocation by linked account,
  enterprise project, cost tags, cost categories, service type.

BILLING SCOPE: Per Member Account and per Enterprise Project.

FINOPS DASHBOARD MAPPING:
- The existing dashboard already uses "Tenants" — this maps to
  Enterprise Projects within a single master account context.
  Keep this as-is for Huawei. The header dropdown shows
  "All Tenants" + individual enterprise project names.
- Sidebar label: "Tenants" (Huawei-specific term stays)
